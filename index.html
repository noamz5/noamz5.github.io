Permits Archiver

A private bot that keeps a tidy Google Drive copy of the delivery permits shared in one Telegram group.

What it does

  1. The bot is a member of a single Telegram group.
  2. When someone posts a photo or a document (such as a PDF permit), the bot saves a copy to a Google Drive folder belonging to the group's operator.
  3. Files are sorted into monthly folders and named after the date, the sender, and the caption, so they are easy to find.

What it does not do

  • It does not store text messages, voice notes, or videos.
  • It does not read any other chats, and it does not read other files in the operator's Google Drive. It can only see the files and folders it created itself.
  • It is not a public service: it is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
