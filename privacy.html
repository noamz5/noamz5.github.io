Privacy Policy

Permits Archiver · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Permits Archiver ("the bot") is a private tool run by the operator of one Telegram group. It copies photos and documents posted in that group to the operator's Google Drive so they are kept safely in one place. This page explains what information it handles.

Information the bot stores

  • Photos and documents posted in the group (for example, pictures or PDFs of delivery permits).
  • For each file: the sender's Telegram display name, the date and time it was posted, and its caption. These are used for the file name and description in Google Drive.
  • Technical identifiers of files already saved, so the same file is not stored twice. This list contains no content.

Information the bot does not store

  • Text messages, voice notes, videos, and stickers.
  • Phone numbers, contact lists, or anything from other chats.

Google account data

The bot connects to the operator's Google account with the narrowest Google Drive permission (drive.file). With this permission it can only create files and see the files and folders it created itself. It cannot read, change, or delete any other files in that Google Drive.

Permits Archiver's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the information is kept and who can see it

  • Files are stored in a Google Drive folder owned by the group's operator. Only people the operator shares that folder with can see them.
  • The bot itself runs on a server controlled by the operator and keeps no copies of the files after uploading them.
  • The information is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Files are kept in Google Drive until the operator deletes them.

Removal requests

To have a file or your name removed from the archive, contact the administrator of the Telegram group. They will delete the file from Google Drive.

Telegram and Google

Messages are sent through Telegram, and files are stored in Google Drive. Their own privacy policies apply to those services: Telegram Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If the bot's behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
